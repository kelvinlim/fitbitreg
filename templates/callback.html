Thank you for sharing your data from you Fitbit device.

We are using your data for research to better understand how sleep, activity and stress impact cognition and behavior. You can remove our access to your data by opening your Fitbit app on your mobile phone and selecting your picture in the top right corner. Next select "Your data in Fitbit", then select "Manage connected apps". Scroll down to the Section for "ResearchData by University of Minnesota" and select "Revoke Access."
